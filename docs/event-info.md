## Challenges and partners

- Three challenge tracks, one each from **BMW Group, Atira, tacto**. Details are revealed at kick-off and in the deep dives.
- Tech partners: **OpenAI** (75 USD API credits per person) and **Entire** (repo tooling + support).
- Challenge and technical questions go through Discord (https://discord.gg/4UDNd5TAg). No guaranteed overnight partner support.

## Rules that affect us

- Team size 3–5. Every member needs their own application on ehl.gg and must be checked in before the captain can select a challenge.
- The captain selects the challenge at https://ehl.gg/. The selection can be changed freely until Sat 13:00.
- Submissions can be updated as often as wanted until Sun 12:00. A submission without Entire is not accepted.
- Pitch slot: strict **six minutes including questions**.

## Entire setup (required)

On the repository's main branch:

1. Run the installer from https://entire.io/install.sh (the page gives `curl -fsSL https://entire.io/install.sh | bash`).
2. Run `entire enable`; the agent confirms whether it is linked.
3. On the next commit the session context is linked. Expected confirmation: `[entire] Pushing 1 checkpoint ref(s) to origin.... done`.

## Prizes

- 1,500 EUR prize pool per challenge; open to all teams.
- 10,000 EUR overall League title; only the 15 invited League finalist teams can reach the main-stage final.
- Mystery and meme side quests, 100 EUR each.
