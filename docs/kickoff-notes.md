## BMW Group (only the second half was recorded)

- Domain: **product management** for a new car. The product manager sits between brand/legacy, markets
  ("my market is different"), engineering (can build anything, not at any price), customers and competition.
- The job described: take a huge amount of information (the predecessor model, competitors, customer and market
  feedback), **condense it, put it in context, prioritize it** and turn it into a set of requirements for
  engineering. Requirements iterate against cost (example: "fully electric, 1,000 km" → cost → "900 km").
- Decisions must be **documented with the context at the time**, because they get revisited years later.
- The ask: build "the smartest tools" for this process.
- Not captured: the concrete task, data, deliverable and judging. No challenge URL known yet. It sounds open-ended
  and jury-judged rather than leaderboard-scored **(unclear)**.

## Atira (matches [challenge-atira.md](challenge-atira.md); additions below)

- Customers build fire trucks, trains, aircraft, machines; the sales process around complex tenders is the
  bottleneck. Atira sells an "AI workforce" for that.
- Speaker's emphasis: **"a single agent will not work"**. Agents working the different systems must exchange
  information because the systems depend on each other (tender quantity changes the ERP price; product code changes
  the discount level). He asked for an agent fleet.
- Ranking as on the site: valid bid → quality → **time and cost**. He expects several teams to reach 100 % quality,
  after which it is "all about optimization", especially time.
- Hard-coded solutions are flagged. After the deadline the agent runs on a private set.
- For the final score they also look at what the team actually did; the EHL submission should describe the
  **development process**, which he called as interesting as the final agent.
- Free unlimited tokens on self-hosted models. Their deep dive covers the challenge and what the team struggles with.
- Atira is hiring in Munich.

## tacto (matches [challenge-tacto.md](challenge-tacto.md); additions below)

- tacto builds procurement software for manufacturers; autonomous negotiation is part of their real product, which
  is why the supplier bots are meant to be realistic.
- Numbers said on stage: **about 20 practice cases today, 50 hidden cases tomorrow**, in a **one-hour live
  tournament at 12:00** shown on the big screen with a live leaderboard. (The repo says 10 hosted practice cases out
  of 19 in the data; the hidden-case count is not in the repo.)
- He showed a replay: the polite agent got a better price than the overconfident one. Tone matters.
- Each supplier has a hidden floor; score is how much of the gap you capture. Squeezing too hard is penalized where
  the relationship or supplier health matters.
- Only the **top 10** of the tournament pitch. "We then select 3 winners" **(unclear** whether that means places
  1–3 or Grand Final slots; the repo says two EHL finalist teams advance).
- Practice arena runs locally and hosted. "You cannot overfit too much": tomorrow's suppliers are different.
